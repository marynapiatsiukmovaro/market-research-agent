# REJECTION RULES

Auto-reject products with:
- poor perceived value relative to price
- difficult logistics (heavy, fragile, oversized, regulated)
- weak or no visual hooks
- low emotional triggers (purely functional, boring)
- excessive saturation (100+ active ads, major brand dominance)
- weak margins (price under $38 or COGS over 35%)
- low paid traffic ad viability
- difficult sourcing (custom manufacturing only, no generic suppliers)
- high return-risk (sizing, fit, color-dependent, electronics)
- complicated customer education required before purchase
- single creative angle only (will die fast under ad fatigue)
- price above $100 without strong existing social proof

## Saturation Levels
- Low: few ads, growing organically → good
- Medium: active ads, multiple sellers, no clear winner → acceptable
- High: dominated by 1-2 major brands with huge budgets → avoid
- Extreme: generic product everyone sells → reject
