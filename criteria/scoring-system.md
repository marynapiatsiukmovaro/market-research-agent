## Scoring Guidance

**Problem-Solving (20):** Does it solve a real, frequent pain? Is the solution obvious?
**Wow-Effect (20):** Would someone stop scrolling? Is there transformation or curiosity?
**Paid Ads (15):** Strong hook? Fast value communication? Impulse-buy capability?
**Emotional Trigger (10):** Fear, desire, embarrassment, pride, convenience?
**Market Size (10):** Broad enough audience for paid traffic scale?
**Margin (10):** Price $39–$79 with COGS under 30%? Room for ad spend?
**Logistics (5):** Lightweight, small, ships easily from China?
**UGC (5):** Before/after dynamics? Creator-friendly? Demo potential?
**Evergreen (5):** Year-round demand? Not seasonal or trend-dependent?

## Recommendation Thresholds
- **85–100** → High Priority Winning Product
- **70–84** → Worth Testing
- **55–69** → Medium Potential (proceed with caution)
- **Below 55** → Reject

Think probabilistically. Never assume certainty.
