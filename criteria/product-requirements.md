# PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS

## Price Range
Sweet spot: **$45–$79** retail (prioritize these)
Extended acceptable: **$39–$100** (with conditions)

- Under $39: margins typically fail with paid traffic CPMs — reject
- $39–$44: acceptable only if margins are confirmed and wow-effect is strong
- $45–$79: ideal range for impulse buy + healthy margins → prioritize
- $80–$100: acceptable only if: perceived value is high, wow-effect is strong, margin is healthy, problem-solving is clear
- $100–$170: Premium tier — acceptable only if: strong existing social proof (reviews, organic virality, established brand presence), high perceived value is obvious on camera, problem-solving is undeniable, and margins support 3x+ ROAS. Use Notion tag "Premium $100–170".
- Above $170: not suitable for cold traffic MVP — reject

## Core Requirements
Prioritize products with:
- strong problem-solving capability (frequent, real pain)
- visible transformation or clear before/after
- wow-factor that stops scroll within 2 seconds
- emotional triggers (pain relief, desire, convenience, pride)
- impulse-buy potential (no long deliberation needed)
- scalable audience (broad demographic, not niche hobby)
- UGC potential (easy to film, demonstrate, review)
- lightweight shipping (under 1kg preferred)
- low return-risk (not size/fit dependent)
- evergreen or long-lasting demand (not pure trend)
- paid traffic survivability (ROAS 2.5+ realistic)
- moderate competition (not dominated, not empty)
- future branding possibility

## Competitor Ad Signal (Required)
Product must show evidence of:
- active ads on Meta Ads Library, OR
- active ads on TikTok Ads Library, OR
- strong organic TikTok/Instagram momentum

No competitor ad activity = no market validation = higher risk.

## Creative Angles (Minimum 3)
Before accepting, identify at least 3 different ad angles:
- e.g. problem/solution, lifestyle, social proof, curiosity, transformation
- single-angle products will face rapid ad fatigue
