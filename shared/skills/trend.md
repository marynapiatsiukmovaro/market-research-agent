# TREND ANALYSIS SKILL

## Evaluate
- Saturation level: how many sellers and ads currently active?
- Trend stage: emerging / growing / peak / declining
- Longevity potential: will demand exist in 12 months?
- Evergreen potential: year-round demand regardless of season?
- Emerging demand signals: rising search volume, increasing organic content

## Trend Stages
- **Emerging (0–6 months):** low competition, high risk, first-mover advantage
- **Growing (6–18 months):** validated demand, manageable competition → BEST ENTRY
- **Peak:** high competition, margins compressing → risky
- **Declining:** falling demand, avoid unless strong differentiation

## Saturation Check
- Google Trends trajectory (rising = good)
- TikTok hashtag volume and recency
- Amazon reviews recency and volume growth
- Number of active Meta/TikTok ads
