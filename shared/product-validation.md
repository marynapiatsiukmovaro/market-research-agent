# PRODUCT VALIDATION WORKFLOW

Trigger only for:
- products scoring 85+ in daily scout
- manually selected products by user

## Validation Checklist

**Sourcing**
- Alibaba: 5+ verified suppliers?
- MOQ acceptable for MVP (20–50 units)?
- Estimated COGS landed under 30% of retail?

**Logistics**
- Weight under 1kg?
- No special shipping restrictions?
- Shipping time acceptable for market?

**Paid Traffic Viability**
- 3+ creative angles identified?
- Hook communicable in under 3 seconds?
- Price in preferred range ($45–$79)? If $39–$44 or $80–$100 — justification provided?
- Realistic ROAS: 2.5x+ achievable?

**Market Validation**
- Competitor ads active on Meta and/or TikTok?
- Multiple Shopify stores selling?
- Amazon reviews with recent dates and volume?

**Saturation**
- Not dominated by major brands?
- Not in obvious decline?
- Entry still realistic for new seller?

## Output
Validation report saved to outputs/high-potential-products/
Flag to user if score upgrades to 85+ after validation.
