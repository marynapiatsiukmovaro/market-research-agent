## How to Update (end of each session)
Add notable rejections only — not every single candidate.
Focus on products that looked promising but failed for a non-obvious reason.
- Date: YYYY-MM-DD
- Product: name or description
- Category: category
- Reason: 1 sentence — why rejected
- Score: if scored, otherwise "filtered"

## Common Rejection Reasons (update as patterns emerge)
- Price under $38 — margins fail paid traffic
- Single creative angle only
- No market validation signal (no ads, no organic)
- Oversaturated — too many active ads, major brand dominance
- High return risk (size/fit dependent)
- Logistics too complex (heavy, fragile, regulated)
