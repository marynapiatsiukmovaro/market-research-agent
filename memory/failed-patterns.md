# FAILED PATTERNS

Patterns to avoid. Update as failures accumulate.

## Recurring Failures (update as discovered)
- [ ] Price under $30 — margins never survive paid traffic
- [ ] Single creative angle — burns out within 2 weeks
- [ ] Generic accessories category — too saturated for cold traffic

## Problematic Categories (update as discovered)
- Generic phone accessories (extreme saturation)
- Basic fitness resistance bands (commoditized)
- Supplements without clear visual result (low UGC, high refund risk)

## Weak Hook Patterns (update as discovered)
- Feature-first ads (no emotional hook)
- Text-heavy creatives without demonstration
- Products that look identical to Amazon generics
