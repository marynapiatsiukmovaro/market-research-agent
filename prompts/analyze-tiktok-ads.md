# ANALYZE TIKTOK ADS PROMPT

Analyze TikTok ads in target category for product signals.

Look for:
- Hook patterns (what stops the scroll?)
- Emotional triggers used (pain, desire, curiosity, transformation)
- Visual patterns (before/after, demo, reaction, lifestyle)
- CTR potential signals (strong opens, clear value in first 3s)
- Product presentation quality (professional vs UGC)
- Price points being tested
- Creative angle diversity (how many different approaches?)

Output:
- Top 3 products identified with signals
- Hook patterns observed
- Gaps or opportunities spotted
- Saturation level assessment
