# SHOPHUNTER ANALYSIS SKILL

Use ShopHunter as supplementary validation only.

## If Available
- Analyze store revenue trajectory (growing = good signal)
- Identify active selling behavior (consistent orders)
- Check how long product has been sold (longevity signal)
- Estimate market traction (multiple stores selling = validated demand)

## Interpretation
- Multiple Shopify stores actively selling = strong market signal
- Revenue growing month-over-month = early/growth stage
- Revenue declining = peak passed, approach with caution
- Single store dominating = either opportunity or heavy competition

## Important
Do NOT reject products solely because ShopHunter data is unavailable.
ShopHunter confirms what other signals suggest — it does not replace them.
