# PRODUCT DISCOVERY SKILL

Goal: Find 5+ viable product candidates per session.

## Process
1. Scan 15–20 candidates from primary sources
2. Apply mandatory filters (fast reject weak ones)
3. Score remaining candidates
4. Output top 5 by score

## Focus
- problem-solving products with strong wow-factor
- paid traffic viability as primary lens
- broad audience (avoid micro-niches)
- emotional triggers that drive impulse decisions
- scalable potential beyond MVP

## Avoid
- low-quality junk (novelty without value)
- oversaturated categories (generic supplements, basic accessories)
- weak hooks (boring products that need explanation)
- poor logistics (heavy, fragile, regulated)
- price outside $39–$100 range

## Source Priority
1. TikTok Ads Library (active spend = market signal)
2. Facebook/Meta Ads Library (same logic)
3. Amazon Movers & Shakers (demand signal)
4. AliExpress/Alibaba trending (sourcing + demand)
5. TikTok organic trending (early signal before saturation)
