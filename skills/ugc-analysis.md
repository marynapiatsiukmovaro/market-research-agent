# UGC ANALYSIS SKILL

## Evaluate
- Content creation potential: can everyday people easily film this?
- Before/after dynamics: is transformation filmable?
- Demonstration clarity: is product use obvious on camera?
- Social proof opportunities: testimonials, reactions, unboxings?
- Creator-friendly: can micro-influencers use it naturally?

## High UGC Potential Signals
- Physical transformation visible on camera
- Reaction-worthy first use (surprise, satisfaction, relief)
- Easy to demonstrate in 15–30 seconds
- Naturally fits lifestyle content (morning routine, fitness, cooking, etc.)
- Strong comment-bait ("where did you get this?")

## Low UGC Potential (flag)
- Benefit is invisible or internal (supplements without visible result)
- Usage is private or embarrassing to film
- Requires professional filming to look good
- No natural storytelling arc
