# SOURCING ANALYSIS SKILL

## Check
- Alibaba: search product, count suppliers (5+ = good availability)
- AliExpress: check listing volume and reviews
- Supplier feasibility: MOQ acceptable for MVP test?
- Shipping simplicity: small packet / ePacket / standard air freight?
- Logistics complexity: any special requirements (battery, liquid, fragile)?
- Estimated COGS: product cost + shipping landed under 30% of retail?

## Green Flags
- 10+ suppliers on Alibaba with 4+ star ratings
- AliExpress listings with 1000+ orders
- Weight under 500g (cheap shipping)
- No dangerous goods classification
- MOQ available at 20–50 units for MVP test

## Red Flags
- Only 1–2 suppliers (supply risk)
- Requires custom manufacturing
- Weight over 2kg (expensive shipping)
- Battery/liquid restrictions
- Fragile = high damage rate = high returns
- COGS lands above 35% of retail price
