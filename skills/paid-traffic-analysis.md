# PAID TRAFFIC ANALYSIS SKILL

## Evaluate for Each Product

**Hook Strength**
- Can the hook be communicated in 0–3 seconds visually?
- Does it create curiosity or immediate desire?
- Would someone stop scrolling?

**CTR Potential**
- Is the product visually striking?
- Is the transformation or benefit immediately obvious?
- Does it trigger an emotional response fast?

**Impulse-Buy Capability**
- Can a viewer decide to buy within 15–30 seconds?
- Is the price point in the impulse range ($39–$79)?
- Is no lengthy research required before purchase?

**Ad Survivability**
- Can 3+ different creative angles be produced?
- Is the product robust across different audiences (age, gender, interest)?
- Can it sustain a 4–8 week test without creative fatigue?

**Economics Check**
- Realistic selling price: $39–$79
- Target COGS (landed): under 30% of retail
- Expected ROAS needed to be profitable: 2.5–3x
- CPM environment: Meta $15–$30, TikTok $8–$20

## Competitor Ad Activity Check
Search Meta Ads Library + TikTok Ads Library:
- Are competitors actively running ads? (positive signal)
- How many active ads? (1–20 = early, 20–100 = growing, 100+ = saturated)
- What hooks are they using? (avoid copying, find gaps)
